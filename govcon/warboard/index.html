Standing orders: a human sends every quote — nothing here submits to SAM.gov or a contracting officer. No certification is ever claimed that the entity doesn't hold. Pricing comes from live supplier quotes, never memory. Offers require an active SAM registration (FAR 52.204-7).
Open blocker — construction & fabrication cards: the SAM registration carries IT/software NAICS only (541511/541512/541519/541690/518210). A set-aside offer needs a small-business size representation under the solicitation's own NAICS, so 236210 · 236220 · 238160 · 327390 · 332311 · 332439 must be added in SAM.gov before any of those cards can be submitted. On trade work, FAR 52.219-14 also requires the prime to self-perform 15% (general) / 25% (specialty) of cost with its own employees — which is why this board tracks pools and fabricated products rather than standalone trade jobs.
