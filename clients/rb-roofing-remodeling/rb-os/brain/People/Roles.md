# Roles

Who at R&B touches the [[Lead-to-Cash]] pipeline, and which application each role leans on.
*(Confirm real org structure on the audit call — see `../../automation-audit.md` §7.)*

| Role | Leans on | What RB-OS does for them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | [[Dashboard]] | One view of the whole business; no spreadsheet wrangling |
| Office / CSR | [[Intake]], [[Triage]], [[Scheduler]] | Leads answered + booked without phone tag |
| Sales rep | [[Estimator]], [[Proposals]] | Instant numbers; proposals + follow-up auto-handled |
| Production / crew lead | [[Jobs]] | Status + materials + homeowner updates handled |
| Bookkeeper | [[Billing]] | Invoices, payment links, dunning automated |

Every role talks to the same [[Allerion Agency]] through one door — they don't learn ten apps.
