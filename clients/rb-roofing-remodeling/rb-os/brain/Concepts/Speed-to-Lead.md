# Speed-to-Lead

The single biggest driver of close rate in home services: how fast a lead gets a real
response. Minutes matter; hours lose deals.

## Why it's the OS's first priority
A lead that lands at 9pm on a Saturday can't wait until Monday. [[Intake]] gives an instant
24/7 response and [[Triage]] routes it immediately — so no lead goes cold. This is why the
P0 phase in `../../whitelabel-ai-blueprint.md` leads with these two.

## Where it shows up
- [[Intake]] — instant auto-response
- [[Triage]] — instant routing
- [[Retention]] — fast, well-timed review/referral asks
- [[Dashboard]] — speed-to-lead is the headline metric
