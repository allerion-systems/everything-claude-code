# Lead-to-Cash

R&B's business as one pipeline: a stranger with a leaky roof becomes a paid, happy customer
who refers the next one.

## The chain
[[Intake]] → [[Triage]] → [[Estimator]] → [[Proposals]] → [[Scheduler]] → [[Jobs]] → [[Billing]] → [[Retention]]

Each link is owned by a specialist hired by the [[Allerion Agency]]; [[Dashboard]] measures the
whole flow. The full stage analysis lives in `../../automation-audit.md`.

## Why model the whole chain
Automating one stage in isolation just moves the bottleneck. The OS owns the chain so clean,
structured data (lead → estimate → proposal → job → invoice) flows end to end without re-keying.
