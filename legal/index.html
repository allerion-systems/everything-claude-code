ALLERION LLC, doing business as Allerion Systems ("we," "our," or "us"), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. 2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number, and company name when you register for an account, request a consultation, or communicate with us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our SaaS products and custom software.
  • To process transactions, manage billing, and verify financial account ownership.
  • To communicate with you regarding project updates, customer support, and system maintenance.
  • To improve our AI integrations, MCP servers, and overall service offerings based on usage patterns.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are restricted from using your data for their own marketing purposes.

  • Business Transfers: If Allerion Systems is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred as part of that business transaction.

5. Data Security and Storage

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. All financial data processed through Stripe Financial Connections is encrypted and handled in compliance with strict financial industry standards. We store your data securely within the United States.
