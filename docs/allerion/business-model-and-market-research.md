# Allerion — Business Model & Market Research

*Decision-oriented brief. Fact, inference, and recommendation are labelled. Every market claim
carries a source. Prepared for J. Allee / Allerion.*

---

## 1. The business in one line

**"AI infrastructure for businesses that want to run themselves."** Allerion sells *recovered
hours and recovered revenue* to ops-heavy SMBs — done-for-you AI automation that connects the
tools they already own (CRM, job software, email, accounting, the shoebox of PDFs) into one
self-reconciling system.

- **Beachhead:** construction & trades (roofing proof point: R&B Roofing), expanding to HVAC,
  plumbing, electrical, field & professional services.
- **Engine:** the DDC library (221 construction-automation skills) + the `digital-5d-brain`
  second-brain system. Each client engagement is also product R&D.
- **Wedge:** SMB-accessible pricing and *plain-English teaching*, in a market that mostly serves
  enterprise.

---

## 2. Market — is the wedge real? (FACT, sourced)

**Construction AI is a fast-growing market with a services-shaped tailwind:**

- AI-in-construction market ~**$6.0B in 2026 → ~$35.5B by 2034, ~24.8% CAGR**; *services*
  revenue is forecast to climb at a **~34% CAGR** because firms increasingly hire integrators to
  tune AI to their own data. [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/artificial-intelligence-in-construction-market), [Fortune Business Insights](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/ai-in-construction-market-109848)
- **Adoption just crossed the chasm: 38% of contractors report *measurable* results from AI in
  2026, up from 17% a year earlier** — the gap is roughly doubling year over year. [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/engineering-and-construction/constructions-ai-fight-moves-to-data-as-38-of-contractors-report-measurable-impact), [Roofing Contractor](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/102046-contractor-ai-adoption-surges-in-2026-report-finds)
- The fight has **moved to *data*** — connecting and standardizing the messy files contractors
  already have. Pilot firms report **30–50% drops in admin hours** (field reports, invoice
  processing, dispatch). [Construction Owners](https://www.constructionowners.com/news/construction-ai-adoption-doubles-in-2026-as-smart-tools-transform-jobsites), [For Construction Pros](https://www.forconstructionpros.com/construction-technology/project-management/article/22963634/servicetitan-industry-report-finds-ai-adoption-accelerating-across-commercial-construction)

**INFERENCE:** The market is at the "early majority" inflection — past proof-of-concept, not yet
saturated by competitors who understand trades. The growth is in *services/integration over
messy data*, which is exactly Allerion's product (Second-Brain Installs + DDC converters). The
window is now; the moat is trade-specific delivery + the brain-as-proof.

| Retainer / managed | **$2,500–$15,000/mo** | [Latenode](https://latenode.com/blog/industry-use-cases-solutions/enterprise-automation/17-top-ai-automation-agencies-in-2025-complete-service-comparison-pricing-guide), [Digital Agency Network](https://digitalagencynetwork.com/ai-agency-pricing/) |
| Senior hourly (boutique) | **$125–$250/hr** | [Latenode](https://latenode.com/blog/industry-use-cases-solutions/enterprise-automation/17-top-ai-automation-agencies-in-2025-complete-service-comparison-pricing-guide) |
| Maintenance | **20–30% of project / yr** | [Latenode](https://latenode.com/blog/industry-use-cases-solutions/enterprise-automation/17-top-ai-automation-agencies-in-2025-complete-service-comparison-pricing-guide) |

**Allerion today:** audit **$1,500** (credited), build **$3K–$8K**, retainer **$500–$2.5K/mo**.

**INFERENCE / FINDING:** Allerion sits **below market on every rung**. That is correct as a
*door-opener* for skeptical, burned-before SMBs — but Allerion is **under-charging on builds,
especially legacy-data jobs** (a shoebox-of-PDFs ingestion is a $15–25K job elsewhere, sold here
for $3–8K). Competitors are mostly enterprise/SaaS-generalist; few combine trade-specific
delivery with this teaching-led, SMB-priced motion. That combination is the differentiator, not
the low price alone.

## 4. The pricing problem to fix (FACT → RECOMMENDATION)

Allerion currently presents **two inconsistent ladders**:

| Storefront (Allerion.io) — *done-for-you service* | digital-5d-brain `products/offers.md` — *self-serve product* |
|---|---|
| Audit $1,500 (credited) | Digital Brain Starter — **Free** |
| Build $3K–$8K fixed | Personal Brain Setup — **$49–$199** |
| Retainer $500–$2.5K/mo | Contractor Brain — **$499–$2,500** |
| CrewVoice $149/mo, Lead Engine, dashboards | Company Brain — **$5,000+** |
| | ALLERION OS — SaaS/API |

A prospect who reads both gets confused. **RECOMMENDATION — collapse into ONE architecture with
two tracks that share the same brain:**

### Unified Allerion offer architecture

**Track A — Self-serve product (low-touch, scalable, lead-gen):**
- **Digital Brain Starter — Free** → lead magnet; a basic personal knowledge graph.
- **Personal Brain — $49–$199** → guided self-setup for an individual.
- **Contractor Brain — $499–$2,500** → estimates, scopes, photos, jobs organized.

**Track B — Done-for-you service (high-touch, high-margin, the core business):**
- **Audit — $1,500**, credited to the build *(hold this — it's the wedge)*.
- **Build — $3K–$8K** for clean/modern-tool jobs; **$8K–$18K for legacy-data / Company Brain
  jobs** *(raise this — see §3)*.
- **Retainer — $500–$2,500/mo**, "your outsourced AI department."

**Add-ons (either track):** CrewVoice ($149/mo), Lead Engine, dashboards, AI Marketplace skills.

**Platform (future):** **ALLERION OS** — the SaaS/API the engagements are R&D for.

The funnel: Free Starter → paid Personal/Contractor Brain → those who want it done *for* them
convert to the Audit → Build → Retainer service track. Self-serve feeds the pipeline; service is
the revenue; OS is the long game.

## 5. ICP & positioning

- **Primary ICP:** owner-operator trades SMB, ~5–50 staff, 4–6 disconnected tools, growing fast
  enough to feel the admin pain, not big enough to have an ops/IT team. Construction/roofing first.
- **Economic buyer = the owner.** Sells on recovered hours + plugged revenue leaks, not features.
- **Positioning vs. competitors:** *"The AI team that actually understands your trade and speaks
  plain English — priced for a contractor, not an enterprise."*
- **Differentiators:** (1) trade-specific delivery (DDC engine), (2) teaching-led sales (not
  dumbed-down), (3) the brain is proof + product + demo, (4) SMB-accessible entry.

## 6. TAM / SAM / SOM (INFERENCE — order-of-magnitude, label as estimates)

- **TAM:** the construction-AI *services* slice of a ~$6B (2026) market growing to ~$35B by 2034,
  with services at ~34% CAGR. [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/artificial-intelligence-in-construction-market)
- **SAM:** English-speaking SMB trades (UK + US) wanting done-for-you automation over their own
  data — a large, underserved subset (most vendors chase enterprise).
- **SOM (next 12 mo):** a realistic boutique run-rate. Illustrative: 2 builds/mo at ~$5K + a
  growing retainer base (10 × ~$1K/mo by month 12) ≈ **~$10K/mo services + ~$10K/mo recurring**
  exiting year one — before self-serve product or CrewVoice. *(Validate against actual close rate
  from the first 10 audits.)*

## 7. Go-to-market

- **Motion:** local/vertical, referral- and proof-led. Land an audit, deliver a visible win,
  turn it into a case study (the `digital-5d-brain` execution rhythm already prescribes this),
  ask for two referrals. Roofing/trades networks compound fast.
- **Top of funnel:** free Digital Brain Starter as the lead magnet; content showing real
  before/after hours saved; the 60-second "answer your own files" demo as the hook.
- **Sales:** the `ai-consulting-playbook` + `/drive-prep` rehearsal + `/close` proposal flow.
- **Proof loop:** every engagement → case study → next sale. The brain is the demo.

---

## 8. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Under-pricing erodes margin** | Raise legacy-data build ceiling toward market (§4); keep audit as the only loss-leader. |
| **Founder-dependent delivery** | Productize via DDC skills + SOPs (second-brain playbook) so delivery is repeatable, not heroic. |
| **"Burned before" skepticism** | Teaching-led sales + 60-second proof + credited audit de-risk the first yes. |
| **Big platforms (ServiceTitan/Procore) ship native AI** | Stay the *integrator across* their tools, trade-specific and local; don't compete as software. |
| **Data privacy concern stalls deals** | Install in the client's own accounts; clear data clause in the service agreement. |

---

## 9. Decisions to make (the "so what")

1. **Adopt the unified offer architecture (§4)** and update Allerion.io + `offers.md` so they tell
   one story.
2. **Raise the build ceiling for legacy-data / Company Brain jobs** to $8K–$18K; hold the $1,500
   audit as the wedge.
3. **Lead every sale with the free Starter + the 60-second proof**, not a feature list.
4. **Instrument the first 10 audits** (close rate, audit→build conversion, hours-saved per job)
   to replace the §6 estimates with real numbers.
