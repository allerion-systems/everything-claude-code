the channel format: **Hook (≤15s) → Teach (one idea, one analogy) → Proof (show it) → Soft CTA.**
Spoken style — short sentences, say it like you'd say it on a roof, not in a boardroom.

## Script 1 — "What AI actually is — explained with a filing cabinet"

**HOOK (0:00–0:12)**
> "Everyone's shouting about AI and most of it sounds like nonsense if you actually run a real
> business. So forget the buzzwords. I'm going to explain what AI actually is using a filing
> cabinet. Ninety seconds. Let's go."

**TEACH (0:12–3:30)**
> "You've got a filing cabinet. Every quote, every job, every photo, every email — it's all in
> there somewhere. The problem isn't that you don't *have* the information. It's that finding it
> takes you twenty minutes and the one person who knows where everything is.
>
> AI is just a filing cabinet that talks back. Same drawers. Same paper. But now you can ask it a
> question — out loud, like a person — and it pulls the answer in seconds. *'What did we quote the
> Hendersons in 2022?'* Ten seconds. Not twenty minutes.
>
> That's it. That's the whole thing. It's not a robot. It's not going to take over. It's a filing
> cabinet you can talk to. And once you've got one, you wonder how you ran the business without it."

**PROOF (3:30–5:00)**
> *(Screen recording.)* "Here's a real folder of old job files. Watch — I ask it the question…
> and there's the answer, pulled straight out of paperwork nobody wanted to dig through."

**CTA (5:00–5:30)**
> "If you want to see this on *your* files, I built a free starter version — link's in the
> description. Next video I'll show you the three jobs this thing does for a contractor without
> being asked. See you there."

## Script 2 — "I gave a roofer's shoebox of PDFs a brain"

**HOOK (0:00–0:12)**
> "This is a shoebox. Well — it's a folder on a laptop, but same thing: hundreds of old PDFs,
> quotes, invoices, photos. Total mess. By the end of this video it's going to answer questions
> like a person. Watch."

**TEACH (0:12–2:30)**
> "Here's the thing most contractors get wrong about AI. They think they need to be tidy *first*.
> Wrong way round. The mess is exactly what it's good at.
>
> All those old files are worth money — they're your history, your pricing, your proof. But right
> now they're dead weight, because nobody can find anything in them. What we do is feed that pile

> ask. *'When did we last do work for this address?'* … *'What did we charge for a flat roof that
> size?'* … *'Pull every job where we used this material.'* Look at that. Twenty seconds, from a
> pile nobody wanted to open."

**CTA (5:30–6:00)**
> "Your business is sitting on the same goldmine — it's just locked in a folder. There's a free
> version to try it on your own files, link below. That's the Higgs field: the invisible thing

## Script 3 — "The 3 jobs AI already does for a contractor"

**HOOK (0:00–0:12)**
> "Three jobs AI is already doing for contractors right now — today, not someday. No coding, no
> robots. You already manage people who do these jobs, so this'll make instant sense."

**TEACH (0:12–4:30)** — *three analogies, one each*
> "**One: the receptionist that never sleeps.** A lead comes in at 9pm Saturday. Right now it sits
> till Monday and maybe goes cold. The AI receptionist answers it, books it, chases the quote — 24/7.
>
> **Two: the filing cabinet that talks back.** I did a whole video on this — every file you've ever
> made, ask it a question, get the answer in seconds.
>
> **Three: the apprentice who's read everything you've ever written.** It drafts the estimate, the
> follow-up email, the job report — in your voice. You check it and send. Saves the evening admin.
>
> Notice what none of those are: none of them replace your crew. They replace the *paperwork* that
> stops you being on the tools."

**PROOF (4:30–5:30)**
> *(Quick montage.)* "Here's the apprentice drafting a follow-up from a real job in about ten
> seconds — and it sounds like the owner, not a robot."

**CTA (5:30–6:00)**
> "Pick the one that'd save you the most Sunday-night admin and start there. Free starter's in the
> description. Subscribe and I'll keep these plain and honest — no hype."
