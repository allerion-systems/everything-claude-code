# Higgs Field — YouTube Channel Strategy

*The public-facing teaching channel that sits on top of Allerion and funnels viewers into it.*

---

## The concept

**Higgs Field.** In physics, the Higgs field is the invisible thing that permeates everything and
gives matter its *mass* — its substance. That's the channel's whole premise:

> **AI is the Higgs field for a trades business — the invisible layer that gives your operation
> substance.** You can't see it, but everything runs heavier and more solid once it's there.

The channel teaches non-technical trades owners what AI actually is and does — in plain English,
with their own world as the example. It's the *authority* play: teach first, sell never (directly).
Allerion is the business; Higgs Field is how the right people find it.

**Tagline options** (pick one when you brand it):
- "AI, explained for people who build things."
- "The invisible field that gives your business mass."
- "AI for the trades — no hype, no jargon."

## Audience

Owner-operators and office managers in construction, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and
field services. Time-poor, skeptical, burned by software before. They don't want a lecture on
neural networks — they want to know *what it does for Tuesday morning.*

## Positioning vs. Allerion (teach → funnel)

| | Higgs Field (channel) | Allerion (business) |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Teach AI to trades, build trust | Install AI, get paid |
| Tone | Plain-English teacher, no pitch | Done-for-you consultant |
| CTA | Free Digital Brain Starter | Audit → Build → Retainer |

Every video teaches one real thing and ends with a **soft** CTA: the free Digital Brain Starter at
allerion.io. No hard selling — the teaching *is* the marketing. This is the "better way of teaching
it" from `skills/ai-consulting-playbook/SKILL.md`, turned into a content channel: outcome-first,
analogies they already own (receptionist / talking filing cabinet / apprentice), and the
60-second "answer your own files" proof as the recurring format.

## Content pillars (rotate these)

1. **Teach one concept** — a single AI idea explained with a trades analogy. (Evergreen, searchable.)
2. **Real before/after** — a job's admin pain → the system fixing it. (Proof; pulls from the
   digital-5d-brain case studies.)
3. **Tool teardown** — "should a roofer use [X]?" honest, trade-specific takes. (Searchable, trust.)
4. **Build-in-public** — building Allerion + the second brain in the open. (Loyalty, story.)

## Cadence (sustainable beats ambitious)

- **1 long video/week** (6–10 min) from pillar 1 or 2.
- **2–3 Shorts/week** — clipped from the long video (reuse `skills/crosspost/SKILL.md` +
  `skills/video-editing/SKILL.md`). One job → one long video → many Shorts → LinkedIn/X posts.
- Batch monthly: film 4 long videos in one session; the content skill fans them out.

## SEO — titles & descriptions

Trades owners search problems, not "AI". Title to the pain + the plain payoff:
- ✅ "Stop re-typing job info between QuickBooks and your job software"
- ✅ "What AI actually does for a roofing company (in plain English)"
- ❌ "Leveraging Generative AI for Construction Workflow Optimization"

**Title pattern:** `[Their pain / their words] — [plain outcome]`.
**Description pattern:** 1 sentence on the pain → 1 sentence on what they'll learn → the free
Digital Brain Starter link (allerion.io) → 3–5 plain-English tags. Pin a comment with the same CTA.

## How this "connects to" Higgs Field / the system

The channel is the top of the funnel; the digital brain is the bottom. The same proof that powers
a sale (answer a client's own files) powers a video. Each engagement → a case study → a video →
new viewers → free Starter signups → audits. The content skill below makes that loop one command.

## Success metrics (first 90 days)

- Consistency: 12 long videos + ~30 Shorts shipped (the only real first-90-days KPI).
- Leading signal: watch-time + Starter-link clicks (not subscriber vanity count).
- Conversion: free Starter signups attributed to the channel; 1+ audit booked from a viewer.
