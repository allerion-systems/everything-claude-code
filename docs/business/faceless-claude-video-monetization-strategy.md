# Faceless Claude Training Video — Monetization Strategy

**Operator:** Allerion Technologies LLC
**Venture:** Faceless (no on-camera presence) educational video content teaching Claude, Claude Code, and AI-agent workflows
**Status:** Strategy v1 — review before execution
**Last updated:** 2026-06-24

> This is a planning document, not a guarantee. RPM/CPM figures, platform payout
> rates, and policies cited here change frequently — re-verify before committing
> spend. Treat the dollar figures as planning anchors, not forecasts.

---

## 1. Thesis

The "AI coding tutorial" niche sits at the intersection of three high-value
properties: a **software/SaaS audience** (high ad CPM), a **fast-moving tool
surface** (constant new content to make), and a **buyer's intent** (viewers are
practitioners with budgets, not casual browsers). Faceless production keeps
marginal cost per video low and makes the channel sellable/delegatable later.

Allerion's edge is that this content can serve **two jobs at once**:

1. A standalone media business (ads + products + sponsorships).
2. A top-of-funnel for Allerion Technologies' consulting / digital-twin / agent
   integration services.

The strategy below builds all four revenue lines in parallel but **sequences**
them so the channel is never dependent on a single income source.

## 3. The four revenue lines

### Line A — Ad / platform revenue (the floor)

- **Primary platform: YouTube long-form (8–14 min).** This is where tech-tutorial
  RPM lives and where search/evergreen discovery compounds. Optimize every video
  past the 8-minute mark to qualify for mid-rolls.
- **Secondary: Shorts / TikTok / Reels** as a *discovery* engine, not a revenue
  engine. Short-form payouts are low; use them to drive subscribers and funnel to
  long-form + products.
- **Realistic ramp:** Ad revenue is a function of (views × RPM). At $8–$12 RPM,
  100k monthly views ≈ **$800–$1,200/mo** — meaningful but not the prize. Treat
  AdSense as the baseline that funds production while the other lines grow.
- **Requirements gate:** YouTube Partner Program eligibility (1,000 subscribers +
  4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months, or the Shorts equivalent).
  Re-confirm current thresholds before counting on monetization.

### Line B — Courses & digital products (the margin)

This is where faceless tech content monetizes far above its ad RPM. Sequence
products from lowest to highest effort:

1. **Lead-magnet freebies** (email capture): a "Claude Code starter kit" — prompt
   templates, a settings.json, a hooks pack. Free, drives the list.
2. **Low-ticket digital products ($9–$49):** prompt packs, agent/skill templates,
   Notion/Obsidian system for managing AI-assisted projects. Sell on **Gumroad**
   (own the customer, ~flat fee) rather than burying margin in a marketplace.
3. **Flagship course ($99–$399):** "Production Claude Code" — the systematic
   workflow (TDD, planning, hooks, MCP, multi-agent). Host on **own platform /
   Gumroad / Teachable** to keep ~90%+ vs. Udemy's marketplace economics.
4. **Cohort or community (recurring):** a paid Discord/Skool with monthly office
   hours and template drops — recurring revenue smooths the ad-revenue volatility.

> **Platform caution:** Udemy's subscription payout share has fallen (~25% → ~15%
> over three years) and it keeps ~63% of marketplace sales. Use Udemy only as a
> *discovery* channel for a stripped version; sell the real flagship where you own
> pricing and the customer relationship.

The product itself can be partly **the ECC plugin ecosystem** — the videos teach
the workflow, the products package it.

### Line C — Sponsorships & affiliate (the multiplier)

- **Affiliate from day one** (works at any audience size): dev tools, hosting,
  AI-adjacent SaaS, and any platform with a referral program the audience already
  buys. Put links in every description; disclose clearly (FTC).
- **Sponsorships at scale** ($20–$50 CPM): once videos clear ~10–25k reliable
  views, run integrations. Target sponsors with **high customer LTV** (dev tools,
  cloud, agent platforms) — they pay the top of the CPM range. One integration on
  a 100k-view video ≈ $2k–$5k, often exceeding a month of AdSense.
- **Packaging:** mirror the existing
  [`metrics-and-sponsorship.md`](./metrics-and-sponsorship.md) playbook — sell
  pilot / growth / strategic tiers and report distribution, adoption, surface, and
  reliability metrics to sponsors.

### Line D — Lead-gen for Allerion (the highest $/viewer)

The videos demonstrate exactly the capability Allerion sells. Convert intent:

- **Soft CTA** in every video: "Allerion Technologies builds production AI-agent
  and digital-twin systems — link below." One closed consulting/integration
  engagement can dwarf a year of ad revenue.
- **Funnel:** video → free starter kit (email) → nurture sequence → "work with
  us" page → discovery call. Track which videos source the best leads and make
  more of those.
- **Content bridge:** occasional case-study-style videos (anonymized) showing the
  *kind* of problem Allerion solves with agents, without exposing client specifics
  or anything restricted (see the Allerion Digital Twin CLAUDE.md rules before
  referencing that project's data).

## 4. Content engine (faceless production system)

Faceless means the *system* is the asset. Standardize it:

- **Format:** screen recording + scripted voiceover (synthetic or recorded VO) +
  clean on-screen code/terminal. No webcam.
- **Pillars (rotate):**
  1. *Tool walkthroughs* — new Claude / Claude Code features, MCP servers, hooks.
  2. *Workflow systems* — TDD with agents, planning, multi-agent orchestration.
  3. *Build-alongs* — ship a small real project end-to-end.
  4. *Comparisons / "how I'd do X"* — high-intent, high-search.
- **Production pipeline (templatized):** idea → outline → script → record screen →
  VO → edit (templated intro/lower-thirds) → thumbnail → SEO metadata → publish →
  repurpose into 2–3 shorts.
- **Cadence:** start at **2 long-form/week**; only scale up once the pipeline is
  repeatable. Consistency beats volume early.
- **Reuse the repo:** every command/skill/agent in ECC is a ready-made video
  topic *and* a product component. The content backlog is effectively the repo's
  feature list.

## 5. 30 / 60 / 90-day plan

**Days 0–30 — Foundation**
- Form/confirm channel + brand under Allerion Technologies LLC; set up Gumroad and
  an email list (lead magnet live).
- Lock the production template (intro, thumbnail style, metadata checklist).
- Publish 8–10 long-form videos (pillars 1–2). Ship the free starter kit.
- Add affiliate links + Allerion soft CTA to every description from video #1.

**Days 31–60 — Traction**
- Hit YPP eligibility; enable AdSense.
- Launch first low-ticket product ($9–$49) to the email list.
- Begin short-form repurposing for discovery.
- Instrument analytics: RPM, retention, subs/video, email opt-in rate, lead
  source per video.

**Days 61–90 — Monetization stack**
- Pre-sell or launch the flagship course ($99–$399).
- Open first sponsorship conversations once a few videos clear ~10–25k views;
  reuse the tiered sponsor packaging.
- Stand up the Allerion "work with us" funnel and review first inbound leads.
- Review unit economics; double down on the best-performing pillar.

## 7. Risks & guardrails

- **Numbers drift:** every RPM/CPM/payout figure here is a moving target —
  re-verify before budgeting.
- **Platform dependency:** don't build only on YouTube. The email list + Gumroad
  customer base are the owned assets; ads are rented reach.
- **Trademark / brand:** "Claude" and "Anthropic" are Anthropic's marks. Position
  as independent educational content ("training for Claude Code"), avoid implying
  official endorsement, and follow Anthropic's brand/usage guidelines.
- **AI-content & disclosure:** follow platform synthetic-media and FTC affiliate/
  sponsorship disclosure rules. Faceless ≠ undisclosed.
- **Client confidentiality:** any Allerion case-study content must respect the
  Allerion Digital Twin data rules (on-property boundaries, ADJACENT labeling,
  inferred-vs-measured claims) and never expose federal-procurement-sensitive
  details.
- **Single-niche concentration:** the AI-tooling space moves fast; keep the
  workflow-systems pillar (durable) heavier than the chase-the-new-feature pillar
  (perishable).
