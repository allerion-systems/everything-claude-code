# Faceless Video Production — Toolchain & Hiring Brief

**For:** Allerion Technologies LLC (Joey Allee) · **Date:** 2026-06-24
**Companion to:** `youtube-revenue-pack.md` (the 5 scripts)

> Reality check on auto-generators: most "faceless video" tools (Pictory, InVideo,
> Fliki's text-to-video) stitch **stock footage** to a script. That's great for
> "Top 10 facts" channels and **wrong** for coding tutorials — your video IS the
> screen recording of Claude Code doing the work. No tool can fake that footage;
> it has to be captured on your machine. Pick one of the two paths below.

---

## Path A — DIY tool stack (cheapest, ~$40–60/mo, full control)

The workflow per video: **record screen → AI voiceover → edit + caption → thumbnail → upload.**

| Step | Tool | Cost | Why |
|------|------|------|-----|
| Screen recording | **Screen Studio** (Mac) or **OBS** (free, any OS) | $0–$89 once | Screen Studio auto-zooms and looks premium; OBS is free and fine |
| AI voiceover | **ElevenLabs** | ~$22/mo | Most natural AI voice in 2026; clone one voice and reuse it forever |
| Edit + captions + remove filler | **Descript** | ~$24/mo | Edit video by editing text; auto-captions; one-click remove silences/"um"s. Best single tool for tutorials |
| Thumbnail | **Canva** | Free | Template once, swap text per video |
| SEO (titles/tags) | **vidIQ** or **TubeBuddy** | Free tier | Title/tag suggestions, scoring |

**Free-est version:** OBS + Descript free + Canva + CapCut. **Recommended:** OBS/Screen Studio + ElevenLabs + Descript + Canva ≈ **$46/mo**.

**The exact "make Video 1" workflow:**
1. Open the script from `youtube-revenue-pack.md` (Video 1).
2. Record your screen in OBS doing the actions the script narrates (CLAUDE.md, /plan, hooks, the self-test loop). ~10 min of raw footage.
3. Paste the script's voiceover lines into ElevenLabs → export MP3 (voice: pick one clear narrator, keep it for every video).
4. Drop footage + MP3 into Descript → align, auto-caption, cut dead air, add the templated intro.
5. Canva thumbnail: big text "80% OF MY WORK" over a terminal screenshot.
6. Upload with the title/description/tags already written in the pack. Pin a comment with the starter-kit link.

> Note: I tested generating the voiceover + thumbnail directly from here — the
> voice rendered but the file couldn't be pulled out of this sandbox, and image
> gen needs a paid Imagen plan. So ElevenLabs (voice) + Canva (thumbnail) on your
> own login is the reliable route. The scripts are done; this is the assembly.

## Path B — Done-for-you (hands-off, you just record the screen)

You record ~10 min of raw screen footage and send it with the script; an editor returns a finished, captioned, thumbnail'd video. Fastest way to actually ship without learning editing.

**Pricing (2026 benchmarks):**
- Per-video freelancer (Upwork/Fiverr): **$40–$150** for retention-focused faceless edits.
- Complex long-form: $150–$300+.
- **Dedicated offshore editor** (e.g. via Somewhere.com / OnlineJobs.ph): **~$1,000–$1,200/mo** for your full upload cadence — the best value once you're posting 2×/week.

**Where:** [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/hire/youtube-video-editors/), [Fiverr "faceless youtube"](https://www.fiverr.com/gigs/faceless-youtube), OnlineJobs.ph, Somewhere.com.

## Recommendation

- **If you want margin and control:** Path A. ~$46/mo, ~2–3 hrs/video at first (faster as you template). Start here for videos 1–4 so you understand the format.
- **If your time is worth more than $50/video:** Path B from video 1 — hire a per-video editor now, move to a dedicated $1,200/mo editor once you're at 2×/week. You only ever record screen + approve.
- **Hybrid (what I'd do):** record screen yourself (you have to anyway), generate the ElevenLabs voiceover yourself (1 min), and hand footage+VO to a cheap per-video editor for assembly. Cheapest path to consistent, polished output.

**Shorts add-on:** for the vertical clips, [Kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/top-ai-tools-faceless-creators-2026/) (~$19/mo) or CapCut can auto-caption and reformat — but the long-form tutorial itself stays screen-recorded.
