## 1. The $10K/month math (how the streams stack)

You do **not** get to $10K on ad revenue alone. You stack five layers on the same audience:

| Layer | Realistic monthly @ scale | What it takes |
|------|---------------------------|----------------|
| **YouTube ads** | $1,200–$2,000 | ~150–200K views/mo @ $8–$12 RPM (8-min+ videos) |

| **Sponsorships** (dev tools, AI SaaS) | $2,000–$4,000 | 1–2 integrations/mo once videos clear ~15–25K views @ $20–$50 CPM |

| **Allerion consulting funnel** (the highest $/viewer) | $2,000–$6,000+ | 1 install every 1–2 months from channel-sourced leads |

**The lever that gets you there fastest is #2 + #5**, not ads. Every video sells the
free starter kit (email capture) → products → and softly funnels into Allerion.
Ad revenue is just the floor that funds production.

**Minimum viable $10K mix (conservative):** $1,500 ads + $3,000 products + $3,000 sponsor + $500 affiliate + $2,000 one consulting retainer = **$10,000.**

## 4. Production pipeline (per video, ~2–3 hrs faceless)

1. **Script** — start from these; tweak to your voice. (~20 min)
2. **Record screen** — OBS, do the actions the script narrates. (~30 min)
3. **Voiceover** — paste script into ElevenLabs, one consistent voice. (~15 min)
4. **Edit** — Descript/CapCut: align VO to screen, auto-captions, templated intro/outro. (~60 min)
5. **Thumbnail** — Canva template, big text from above. (~15 min)
6. **Publish** — title + description block + tags; pin a comment with the starter-kit link. (~10 min)
7. **Repurpose** — clip 2–3 vertical Shorts from the best 30–60s moments.
