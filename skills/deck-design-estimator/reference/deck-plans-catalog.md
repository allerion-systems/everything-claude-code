# Deck Plans Catalog

Reference of common deck plan archetypes. Pick three from this list (varying on ≥2 axes) when generating design options.

Axes of variation:
- **Shape**: rectangle, L-shape, T-shape, octagon, wrap-around, multi-level
- **Elevation**: low (<30"), medium (30-48"), high (>48"), multi-level
- **Attachment**: ledgered to house, freestanding (floating), partial-attach
- **Special use**: pool surround, porch (covered), hot tub bay

## Low-elevation plans (<30" above grade)

Often permit-exempt; simpler footings. Best for ranch homes, walk-out sliders, or flat lots.

### LP-1: Simple Rectangle
- 12' × 16' (192 sq ft) up to 16' × 20' (320 sq ft)
- Ledgered, single beam, 6 footings
- Best for: dining + grill, tight budgets, first-time DIY
- Build time: 2-3 days for crew of 2

### LP-2: L-Shape with Bench Wall
- 16' × 20' main + 8' × 8' bump-out (320 + 64 = 384 sq ft)
- Bench acts as low railing on the unattached side
- Best for: defined dining + lounge zones, low elevation only
- Build time: 4-5 days

### LP-3: Floating Octagon
- 12' or 14' across (113 or 154 sq ft)
- 8 footings around perimeter, freestanding
- Best for: secondary deck under a tree, fire-pit zone
- Build time: 3 days

### LP-4: Wrap-Around (single-story)
- L-shape that turns the corner of the house
- 16' × 8' along back + 6' × 12' along side (128 + 72 = 200 sq ft)
- Best for: ranch homes with kitchen + LR exits
- Build time: 4 days

## Medium-elevation plans (30-48" above grade)

Guardrail required, typically permit required. Stair count: 4-6 risers.

### MP-1: Standard Rectangle with Stairs
- 14' × 20' (280 sq ft) or 16' × 24' (384 sq ft)
- Ledgered, one beam, perimeter rail, 4-rise stair
- Best for: most colonial/traditional homes with back door above grade
- Build time: 4-5 days

### MP-2: Two-Level Stepped
- Upper 12' × 16' (192 sq ft) + lower 12' × 14' (168 sq ft) = 360 sq ft
- 2 risers between levels (no stair rail required at <30" intermediate)
- Best for: zone separation (dining up, lounge down) without going huge
- Build time: 6-7 days

### MP-3: L-Shape with Stair Landing
- 16' × 20' main + 6' × 8' landing at stair = 320 + 48 = 368 sq ft
- Stair runs perpendicular off the landing — easier than a long single run
- Best for: when stairs need to land on a specific path
- Build time: 5 days

### MP-4: Wrap-Around with Two Stairs
- Wraps two sides of the house; stairs at each end
- 18' × 8' + 6' × 14' (144 + 84 = 228 sq ft)
- Best for: corner houses, dual-exit lifestyle
- Build time: 5-6 days

## High-elevation plans (>48" above grade)

42" guardrail in many jurisdictions; engineered lateral bracing; long stair run.

### HP-1: Tall Rectangle with Stair Run
- 14' × 20' (280 sq ft) at deck level
- Stair: ~10-12 risers in a single run, with landing if >12 risers
- 6 footings minimum; posts braced
- Best for: walk-out basements turned into upper-level decks
- Build time: 6-7 days
- **Flag**: lateral load connection per IRC R507.9.2

### HP-2: Tall Multi-Level (upper + landing + lower)
- Upper 14' × 16' (224 sq ft) → 6-rise to mid-landing 8' × 8' (64 sq ft) → 4-rise to grade
- Visually breaks the tall mass; safer stairs
- Best for: 2-story homes where a single straight stair would be 14+ risers
- Build time: 8-10 days
- **Flag**: structural engineer recommended

## Specialty plans

### SP-1: Pool Deck Surround
- Wraps an above-ground pool (12-24 ft diameter)
- Frame must hold gravity load + lateral pool load; freestanding
- Surface 4" below pool top rail
- Best for: above-ground pool conversion to in-ground feel
- Build time: 6-8 days

### SP-2: Hot Tub Bay
- 16' × 20' main deck + 8' × 8' reinforced bay
- Bay framed for 100+ PSF live load (hot tub + water + occupants)
- Best for: dedicated spa zone with screening
- Build time: 6 days
- **Flag**: confirm hot tub model weight; may need engineer

### SP-3: Covered Porch (deck + roof)
- 12' × 20' deck with 4-post roof system
- Roof: shed or gable, tied to house with ledger
- Best for: rain protection; outdoor living year-round in temperate climates
- Build time: 10-14 days
- **Flag**: roof load adds to footings; engineered structure

### SP-4: Modern Floating Platform
- 16' × 16' freestanding, ipe or premium composite, no railing (because <30")
- Cable or no railing for clean lines; integrated bench
- Best for: modern/contemporary homes, minimalist aesthetic
- Build time: 5 days

## Picking three for a homeowner

Aim for genuine variety. Examples of good triplets:

| If site is... | Option A | Option B | Option C |
|---------------|----------|----------|----------|
| Flat lot, walk-out slider, ranch | LP-1 simple rectangle PT | LP-2 L-shape composite | SP-4 floating platform hardwood |
| 36" above grade, colonial, family of 4 | MP-1 rectangle PT | MP-3 L w/ landing composite | MP-2 two-level premium composite |
| Walk-out basement, 2-story | HP-1 tall rectangle PT | HP-2 multi-level composite | HP-2 multi-level + pergola hardwood |
| Pool in backyard | LP-3 floating octagon PT | MP-1 rectangle composite | SP-1 pool surround premium |
| Hot tub on the wishlist | LP-1 rectangle PT no tub | MP-2 two-level + tub bay composite | SP-3 covered porch + tub premium |

Bad triplet: three rectangles in three different composites. That's three colors of paint, not three designs.

## Standard dimensions to plan around

| Function | Minimum footprint |
|----------|-------------------|
| Dining table for 4 + chairs | 10' × 10' |
| Dining table for 6-8 + chairs | 12' × 12' |
| Standard grill + 3 ft clearance | 4' × 6' |
| Conversation seating (4 chairs + table) | 10' × 12' |
| Chaise lounge × 2 + side table | 8' × 10' |
| Standard hot tub (7' × 7') + 3 ft access on 2 sides | 10' × 13' |
| Outdoor kitchen run (sink + grill + counter) | 3' × 10' |
| Stair run, 36" wide, 4 risers | 36" wide × ~5' run |
| Stair run, 36" wide, 10 risers | 36" wide × ~13' run + landing |

## Sizing rule of thumb

Deck footprint should be no more than 20% of the house's heated square footage. A 2,000 sq ft house → ~400 sq ft deck max for proportional balance. Going bigger isn't wrong; it just shifts the visual weight outdoors.
