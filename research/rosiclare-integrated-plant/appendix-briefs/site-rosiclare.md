## Brief: site-rosiclare

### Key facts

- **Mining history / cessation.** The Illinois-Kentucky Fluorspar District (IKFD) supplied >90% of US fluorspar at its peak; the last two operating Illinois mines — Ozark-Mahoning's Annabel Lee (opened Nov 1984) and Minerva No. 1 — ceased production on Nov 29, 1995, ending US primary fluorspar mining ([mindat Annabel Lee](https://www.mindat.org/loc-3752.html); [ISGS Circular 604 production history](https://ilmineswiki.web.illinois.edu/wiki/Circular_604/Production_History)). Cumulative district output is ~12.5 Mt of refined fluorspar since the 1800s ([CleanTech](https://cleantechctv.com/fluorspar-history-kentucky/)). Fluorite has been Illinois' official state mineral since 1965 ([ISGS Geobit 4](https://library.isgs.illinois.edu/Pubs/pdfs/geobits/geobit4.pdf)).
- **Critical-mineral status.** Fluorspar is on the 2025 US Critical Minerals List (60 minerals) ([USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/news/science-snippet/interior-department-releases-final-2025-list-critical-minerals)) with **100% net import reliance every year 2020–2024**; 2024 imports ~440 kt (400 kt acid-grade), import unit value $470/t acid-grade, sources (2020–23) Mexico 62%, Vietnam 14%, South Africa 9%, China 8%; tariff-free under HTS 2529.21/2529.22; 22% domestic depletion allowance; no government stockpile ([USGS MCS 2025 fluorspar](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-fluorspar.pdf)).
- **Residual domestic supply today.** The only 2024 US fluorspar sales came from **Cave-In-Rock, IL (Hardin County)** — byproduct stockpiles from Hastie Mining's limestone quarry ([USGS MCS 2025](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-fluorspar.pdf); [Hastie profile](https://orsb-illinois.com/featured-business/hastie-mining-trucking/)). US HF is made only in Louisiana and Texas; ~40 kt/yr fluorosilicic acid (≈65 kt CaF2-equivalent) is recovered from three phosphoric-acid plants, and an Aurora, NC company began making anhydrous HF from FSA in 2024 (same MCS source).
- **Restart attempts.** Hastie Mining & Trucking (Rosiclare/Cave-in-Rock, in fluorspar since 1965) invested >$3M reopening the old Cerro mine/mill near Salem, KY and has pursued the Klondike II mine in Livingston County, KY since 2010 ([Times Leader](https://www.timesleader.net/news/fluorspar-mining-returns-to-crittenden-livingston-counties/article_d0a3178b-28f8-57f8-8050-716be9d473f4.html); [IMFORMED](https://imformed.com/fluorine-focus-new-alternative-source-developments/)). Note: "American Fluorite" in Rosiclare is the American Fluorite Museum on the former Rosiclare Lead & Fluorspar Mining Co. mill site atop the Rosiclare shaft — not a producing company ([CLUI](https://clui.org/ludb/site/american-fluorite-museum)).
- **Hicks Dome.** ISGS estimates **12–65 Mt of critical-minerals ore** (fluorspar + heavy REEs: Dy, Sc, Y) at Hicks Dome on the Pope–Hardin county line, "likely one of the largest fluorspar reserves in North America," near Ohio/Mississippi river confluence ([ISGS Hicks Dome](https://isgs.illinois.edu/research/critical-minerals/hicks-dome/)). CleanTech Vanadium acquired 7,180 acres of IKFD fluorspar projects for US$4M ([Newsfile](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/262229/CleanTech-Acquires-Significant-Package-of-Fluorspar-Projects-Totaling-7180-Acres-for-US4000000-in-IllinoisKentucky-Fluorspar-District)); American Lithium Minerals acquired 1,358 gross acres around Hicks Dome (2022) ([Electrification Coalition IL factsheet](https://electrificationcoalition.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Illinois_Minerals-Factsheet-for-EC_2023-10.pdf)).
- **Federal reassessment underway.** USGS/ISGS Earth MRI is investing ~$525,000 to map the IKFD and Midwest Permian Ultramafic District, with 2019 airborne geophysics ([USGS BIL Illinois](https://www.usgs.gov/special-topics/bipartisan-infrastructure-law-investments/news/bipartisan-infrastructure-law-funds-3)); KGS built a 3D model of the western Kentucky district ([UKnowledge](https://uknowledge.uky.edu/kgs_data/14/)). USGS's $2.5M BIL mine-waste program funds ISGS's "Characterization of mine waste **tailings piles** of the Illinois-Kentucky Fluorspar District" ([USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/news/state-news-release/usgs-awards-25-million-state-geological-surveys-mine-waste-projects)) — direct federal validation of tailings-reprocessing feedstock at/near the proposed site.
- **Barge access.** Rosiclare began as Pell's Landing (1832) and historically railed mine output to Ohio River barges ([Digital Research Library of Illinois History](https://drloihjournal.blogspot.com/2016/12/the-history-of-rosiclare-illinois-in.html); [HMDB "Mine It, Mill It, Ship It"](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=206198)). The reach lies upstream of Smithland Locks & Dam (Ohio River mile 918.5, twin 1,200 ft × 110 ft locks — largest class on the river), 62.5 mi above the Mississippi confluence ([USACE Louisville](https://www.lrl.usace.army.mil/Missions/Civil-Works/Navigation/Locks-and-Dams/Smithland-Locks-and-Dam/)).
- **Timber/biomass.** Shawnee National Forest: ~289,000 acres of oak-hickory forest surrounding Hardin County; commercial timber harvest is an active management tool ([USFS Shawnee](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/shawnee); [Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2023-09-shawnee-national-forest-debate-swirls.html)). County-level logging/mill residue tonnage is queryable via USFS Timber Products Output tools ([TPO](https://research.fs.usda.gov/products/dataandtools/timber-products-output-tpo-interactive-reporting-tool)).
- **Grid.** Hardin County is served by SouthEastern Illinois Electric Cooperative (~23,932 accounts, 3,563 line-miles across 10 counties; wholesale power via Southern Illinois Power Cooperative; ~1.37 outages/customer-yr, 135.6 min avg) ([SEIEC](https://seiec.com/); [FindEnergy](https://findenergy.com/providers/southeastern-illinois-electric-cooperative/); [SIPC](https://sipower.org/members/)).
- **Incentives.** Illinois EDGE: credit equal to 50% of new-job withholding, **75% in underserved areas** (Hardin County likely qualifies), +10% for training ([DCEO EDGE](https://dceo.illinois.gov/expandrelocate/incentives/edge.html)); REV Illinois for EV/renewables supply chain: min $2.5M investment/50 jobs ([Illinois EDC](https://www.illinoisedc.org/resources/incentives/)); Enterprise Zone program: sales-tax exemption on building materials/manufacturing equipment and state utility-tax exemption ([DCEO EZ](https://dceo.illinois.gov/expandrelocate/incentives/taxassistance/enterprisezone.html)); CEJA (2021) Illinois Shines pre-pays 15–20 yr of SRECs, often 30–50% of solar capex ([Illinois Shines](https://illinoisshines.com/about/); [Mayer Brown on CEJA](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2021/10/illinois-enacts-comprehensive-climate-and-clean-energy-legislation)); USDA REAP grants reached $1M/50% cost-share for renewable systems, but **grant window paused Mar 31, 2026** pending new rules (loan guarantees still open) ([USDA RD](https://www.rd.usda.gov/inflation-reduction-act/rural-energy-america-program-reap); [Federal Register NOFO](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/10/16/2024-23854/notice-of-funding-opportunity-for-the-rural-energy-for-america-program-for-fiscal-years-2025-2026)). Mining is regulated under the Illinois Fluorspar and Underground Limestone Mines Act, 225 ILCS 710 ([ILGA](https://ilga.gov/Legislation/ILCS/Articles?ActID=1389&ChapterID=24)). No Illinois-specific "critical minerals" statute was verifiable; state activity runs through ISGS programs.

### State of the art

Domestic fluorspar activity is limited to (1) Hastie's byproduct stockpile sales at Cave-In-Rock, (2) Ares Strategic Mining's Lost Sheep mine (Spor Mountain, UT) with a $19M, 500 tpd Delta, UT flotation plant targeting 90% met-spar and 97% acid-spar — billed as North America's first modern domestic processing plant ([Ares](https://www.aresmining.com/lost-sheep-mine); [ChemAnalyst](https://www.chemanalyst.com/NewsAndDeals/NewsDetails/utah-to-unveil-north-americas-first-domestic-fluorspar-processing-plant-18857)), and (3) fluorine recovery from phosphate FSA (Aurora, NC AHF startup, 2024) plus DOE byproduct aqueous HF at Paducah, KY/Portsmouth, OH ([USGS MCS 2025](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-fluorspar.pdf)). Public-sector science (Earth MRI mapping, ISGS tailings characterization, KGS 3D modeling) is actively de-risking the IKFD, but no company has integrated district mining/tailings with downstream fluorochemistry.

### Gaps & pain points (candidate white space)

1. **Tailings-to-acid-spar beneficiation:** ISGS is only now characterizing IKFD tailings piles; no published process claims cover sensor-sorted/flotation upgrading of weathered fluorspar gob co-recovering barite/zinc/REE — a disclosure family could pair with the USGS mine-waste dataset ([USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/news/state-news-release/usgs-awards-25-million-state-geological-surveys-mine-waste-projects)).
2. **No US acid-grade→fluorochemical integration outside LA/TX:** all HF capacity is Gulf Coast; a river-barge-fed CaF2→HF/fluorochemical micro-plant at Rosiclare (Smithland pool, jumbo-lock class) is unoccupied territory ([MCS 2025](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-fluorspar.pdf); [USACE](https://www.lrl.usace.army.mil/Missions/Civil-Works/Navigation/Locks-and-Dams/Smithland-Locks-and-Dam/)).
3. **Biochar/pyrolysis coupling:** hardwood-residue pyrolysis gas/heat driving spar drying, pelletizing (biochar as binder/reductant), or activated-carbon capture of fluoride effluent has no incumbent in the district; Shawnee-adjacent residue supply is documented but unmonetized ([USFS TPO](https://research.fs.usda.gov/products/dataandtools/timber-products-output-tpo-interactive-reporting-tool)).
4. **Expired FSA→AHF art (1977–79)** invites modernized, energy-integrated (waste-heat/CHP) conversion claims.
5. **Weak rural grid** (co-op distribution, purchased wholesale power) makes behind-the-meter syngas CHP/microgrid claims commercially and technically motivated ([FindEnergy](https://findenergy.com/providers/southeastern-illinois-electric-cooperative/)).
6. **Incentive friction:** REAP grants paused (2026); Hardin County-specific enterprise-zone status unverified in DCEO listings — site the project to capture EDGE 75% underserved-area credit and pursue zone designation ([DCEO](https://dceo.illinois.gov/expandrelocate/incentives/taxassistance/enterprisezone.html)).
