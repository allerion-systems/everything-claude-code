## Brief: fluorochemicals

### Key facts

- The US has been 100% net-import-reliant on fluorspar since 1995; 2025e acid-grade imports were 360,000 t at an average CIF unit value of $470/t (up from $322/t in 2021). Import sources 2021–24: Mexico 64%, Vietnam 12%, China 10%, South Africa 9% (https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-fluorspar.pdf).
- USGS explicitly notes the only current domestic fluorspar activity is sales from stockpiles at a limestone quarry in **Cave-In-Rock, IL — Hardin County, the same district as Rosiclare** — plus a Utah mine under construction (https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-fluorspar.pdf).
- All US anhydrous HF is made in Louisiana and Texas; one company (Aurora, NC) makes HF from fluorosilicic acid (FSA). In 2025 ~45,000 t of FSA (~73,000 t CaF2-equivalent) was recovered from three phosphoric acid plants; US phosphate-rock reserves hold ~72 Mt fluorspar-equivalent (https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-fluorspar.pdf).
- Conventional HF: acid-grade CaF2 + H2SO4 in externally heated horizontal rotary kilns, 200–250°C, 30–60 min residence, CaSO4 byproduct; rotary-kiln HF dates to US1,812,770 (1931) and remains state of the art (https://www.neonickel.com/technical-resources/rotary-kilns-used-in-the-manufacture-of-hydrofluoric-acid-hf-2; https://patents.google.com/patent/EP1167286B1/en).
- Koura (Orbia Fluor & Energy Materials, ex-Mexichem Fluor) won a **$100M DOE Bipartisan Infrastructure Law award (Oct 2022)** to build the **first US LiPF6 plant** at its St. Gabriel, LA fluorochemical site: up to 10,000 MT/yr, enough for ~1M EVs/yr, ~80 new jobs, targeted operational 2026 (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/orbias-fluorinated-solutions-business-koura-receives-100m-us-department-of-energy-award-301654096.html; https://www.orbia-fem.com/media-center/orbia-100m-doe-award-lipf6-manufacturing-plant-louisiana). Koura proposed $800M across two Iberville Parish battery-chain projects (https://www.opportunitylouisiana.gov/news/koura-proposes-800-million-investment-in-two-ev-battery-supply-chain-projects-in-iberville-parish).
- Notably, Orbia F&EM **licensed LiPF6 technology from Kanto Denka Kogyo (Japan)** for the North American market — i.e., the flagship US project runs on imported process IP (https://www.kouraglobal.com/north-american-lithium-ion-battery-supply-chain-boosted-through-localization-of-lithium-hexafluorophosphate-lipf6-production/).
- China holds ~95% of global LiPF6 production; Do-Fluoride (DFD) built capacity to ~100,000 t/yr, with Tinci, Yongtai, and Jiangsu peers dominating (https://www.marketgrowthreports.com/market-reports/lithium-hexafluorophosphate-lipf6-market-113468).
- HF-free breakthrough: Gouverneur (Oxford) ball-milled acid-grade fluorspar with K2HPO4 to give "Fluoromix" (K3(HPO4)F + K2−xCay(PO3F)a(PO4)b), forging S–F and C–F bonds in >50 fluorochemicals, bypassing HF entirely — Science 381, 302–306 (2023) (https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adi1557).
- Follow-up: **one-step HF-free synthesis of LiF, NaF, KF from fluorspar** — mechanochemical reaction of AGF with alkali (hydr)oxides + TiO2, sequestering Ca as CaTiO3 to prevent back-reaction — JACS 147, 6338–6342 (2025) (https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jacs.4c16608); reviewed in Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. (doi 10.1002/anie.202500255) (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/anie.202500255).
- Oxford spinout **FluoRok** (founded 2022) raised £7.7M ($9.8M) in Sept 2024 (BGF, Green Generation Fund, Volta Energy Technologies) to scale HF-free fluorinating reagents and **LiPF6** (https://www.fluorok.com/news/fluorok-raises-7-7m-9-8m-to-transform-the-safety-and-sustainability-of-fluorochemical-production/).
- Safety driver: the US Chemical Safety Board opened repeat investigations into HF releases at Honeywell Geismar, LA — described as the world's largest HF plant (https://www.csb.gov/us-chemical-safety-board-launches-investigation-into-another-release-of-toxic-hydrofluoric-acid-at-honeywell-facility-in-geismar-louisiana/; https://cen.acs.org/safety/industrial-safety/Chemical-safety-board-investigates-Honeywell/102/i19).

### State of the art

Industrial fluorine chemistry is still a two-step funnel: (1) rotary-kiln sulfation of acid-grade CaF2 to anhydrous HF (endothermic, 200–250°C, CaSO4/anhydrite byproduct) (https://www.neonickel.com/technical-resources/rotary-kilns-used-in-the-manufacture-of-hydrofluoric-acid-hf-2); (2) HF-derived downstream salts. Battery-grade LiPF6 is classically made by dissolving LiF in anhydrous HF and sparging PF5; variants include the Ulsan/Foosung route (PCl5 + HF → PF5, reacted with LiCl in HF at −30°C to RT with an F2 polish; ≥90% yield, ≥99.8% purity, ≤20 ppm moisture — US6,387,340B1) (https://patents.google.com/patent/US6387340B1/en), LiF + PF5 in liquid SO2 (99.5% yield, ≥99.90% purity), direct gas–solid F2/LiF-P synthesis (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/326870007_Synthesis_of_LiPF_6_via_a_direct_gas-solid_reaction), solid-state thermal synthesis from Li source + diammonium phosphate at 150–600°C (US6,824,754) (https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/6824754), and Chinese microchannel-reactor continuous processes (CN110683563B) (https://patents.google.com/patent/CN110683563B/en). FSA-to-AHF (ammonia digestion → NH4F/NH4HF2 → HF) is technically proven and reviewed in Frontiers in Chemistry (2024) but commercially marginal — one US plant (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/chemistry/articles/10.3389/fchem.2024.1372981/full). LiFSI mass production began with Nippon Shokubai (2013, "IONEL"), scaled 300 → 2,000 t/yr at Ichikawa (2023), plus an Arkema JV at Pierre-Bénite, France targeted end-2025 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210113005985/en/NIPPON-SHOKUBAI-Enhancement-of-Facilities-and-Patent-Rights-for-Lithium-ion-Battery-Electrolyte-IONEL-LiFSI; https://www.arkema.com/global/en/media/newslist/news/non-global/countries/japan/2022/20220531-nippon-shokubai-and-arkema-a-strategic-partnership-01/). Battery-grade NaPF6 for sodium-ion currently piggybacks on LiPF6-style chemistry; a cleaner route (NH4PF6 + Na metal in THF, avoiding NaF/hydrolysis impurities) was shown at multigram scale (Angew. Chem. 2021) (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/anie.202111215). The frontier is HF-free mechanochemistry: Fluoromix (Science 2023) and TiO2-mediated direct CaF2→LiF/NaF/KF (JACS 2025).

### Gaps & pain points (candidate white space)

1. **No indigenous US LiPF6 process IP** — the only US plant licenses Japanese (Kanto Denka) technology; a domestically owned fluorspar-to-electrolyte-salt process chain is open ground (https://www.kouraglobal.com/north-american-lithium-ion-battery-supply-chain-boosted-through-localization-of-lithium-hexafluorophosphate-lipf6-production/).
2. **Scale-up of HF-free mechanochemistry**: Science/JACS routes are batch ball-mill, hours-long; continuous reactive extrusion / twin-screw mechanofluorination, in-line Ca-sequestrant regeneration (CaTiO3 recycle), and energy integration with on-site CHP/waste heat are unclaimed engineering space (https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jacs.4c16608).
3. **Direct fluorspar→battery-grade LiF/NaPF6**: JACS 2025 demonstrates LiF/NaF/KF but not battery purity specs (moisture ≤20 ppm, ppb metals); purification trains — including activated-carbon adsorption, a natural coupling to the site's biochar/AC stream — are patentable (https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jacs.4c16608).
4. **Small modular / inherently safer HF or HF-minimized plants**: repeat CSB investigations at Geismar make "no bulk HF inventory" process claims both defensible and ESG-financeable (https://www.csb.gov/us-chemical-safety-board-launches-investigation-into-another-release-of-toxic-hydrofluoric-acid-at-honeywell-facility-in-geismar-louisiana/).
5. **FSA co-feed**: only one US FSA-to-HF plant despite ~73,000 t/yr CaF2-equivalent FSA and 72 Mt fluorspar-equivalent in US phosphate reserves; hybrid fluorspar+FSA flowsheets and modern rework of expired FSA patents (US4,144,315 era) are open (https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-fluorspar.pdf; https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/chemistry/articles/10.3389/fchem.2024.1372981/full).
6. **Byproduct valorization**: CaSO4/anhydrite, CaTiO3, and synthetic fluorspar recycled from pickling/alkylation wastes into battery salts (USGS notes impurities constrain current recycling) — integrated-site claims coupling these to co-located manufacturing (https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-fluorspar.pdf).
7. **Sodium-ion salts**: no dedicated Western NaPF6/NaFSI producer; impurity-controlled scalable NaPF6 routes remain academic (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/anie.202111215).
8. **Lapsed-IP arbitrage**: the ceased Necsa perfluorinated-solvent LiPF6 family (WO2019186481A1) offers a validated non-HF-solvent starting point for new continuation-style filings with site-specific integration claims (https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2019186481A1/en).
