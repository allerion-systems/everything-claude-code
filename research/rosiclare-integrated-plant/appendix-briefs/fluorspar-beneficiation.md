## Brief: fluorspar-beneficiation

### Key facts

- Acid-grade fluorspar (acidspar) must run ≥97% CaF2 with typical impurity caps of <1–1.5% SiO2, <1.5% CaCO3, S <0.03–0.1%, As ~10–12 ppm, Pb <100–550 ppm; arsenic carries through to HF (500 ppm As in spar → up to 900 ppm in HF vs. accepted 10–50 ppm) ([EPA fluorspar/HF background doc](https://archive.epa.gov/epawaste/nonhaz/industrial/special/web/pdf/id4-hfa.pdf); [US5047226 arsenic-removal patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5047226A/en)).
- The US is ~99% import-dependent: buyers imported 440 of 445 tonnes consumed/sold in 2024, and Ares Strategic Mining's Lost Sheep mine (Spor Mountain, Utah) is the only permitted US fluorspar mine, restarting under a $169M DoD supply deal ([Northern Miner](https://www.northernminer.com/news/ares-restarts-us-fluorspar-output-after-pentagon-deal/1003887437/)). Fluorspar is on the 2025 US critical minerals list ([USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/news/science-snippet/interior-department-releases-final-2025-list-critical-minerals)).
- The Illinois-Kentucky Fluorspar District (>2,500 km²) was the most productive US fluorspar area from 1870; run-of-mine ore was typically >30% fluorite, 1–14% sphalerite, 1–5% galena, with byproduct Zn, Pb and barite ([GeoScienceWorld](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/segweb/books/book/1931/chapter/107705976/Geology-of-the-Illinois-Kentucky-Fluorspar); [USGS PP 970](https://pubs.usgs.gov/pp/0970/report.pdf)). Mining ended when Ozark-Mahoning shut Minerva No. 1 (ore milled at Rosiclare) at end-January 1996 ([mindat](https://www.mindat.org/loc-3754.html); [Rosiclare history](http://www.cityofrosiclare.com/Our-History.html)).
- ATSDR's health consultation on the Minerva No. 1 site found barium, cadmium and lead above comparison values in on-site soil, tailings waste and sediment — the district's legacy tailings are metal-bearing ([ATSDR](https://www.atsdr.cdc.gov/HAC/pha/MinervaMine1/MinervaMineHC050407.pdf)).
- Earth MRI work flags the district for critical minerals beyond fluorite: Ge-bearing sphalerite, Zn, barite and REE, with Hicks Dome breccias enriched in REE, Y, Th, F, Ba, Ti, Nb, Be; new airborne geophysics covers MO/IL/KY ([KGS Phase I report](https://www.uky.edu/KGS/news/2022_critical_mineral.php); [ISGS critical minerals](https://isgs.illinois.edu/research/critical-minerals/); [USGS Earth MRI Illinois](https://www.usgs.gov/news/state-news-release/earth-mri-funds-critical-minerals-projects-illinois)).
- Local incumbent: Hastie Mining & Trucking (Rosiclare) produces ~10,000 tpa byproduct metspar from its Cave-in-Rock limestone quarry and has long pursued the Klondike II vein mine (Livingston Co., KY; ≥1.6 Mt at ~60% CaF2) without reaching production ([IMFORMED](https://imformed.com/fluorine-focus-new-alternative-source-developments/)).
- Classic acidspar reagent suite: fatty-acid (oleic/tall-oil) collector + soda ash pH control + sodium silicate silica/slimes depressant + quebracho tannin for calcite, with multi-stage (5–8) cleaning ([911 Metallurgist flowsheet](https://www.911metallurgist.com/blog/fluorspar-beneficiation-process-flotation-plant/)). Fluorite–calcite separation remains the hardest problem because both expose Ca sites that adsorb fatty acids non-selectively ([Springer, J. Cent. South Univ. 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11771-026-6220-7); [Adv. Colloid Interface Sci. review 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0001868621000233)).

### State of the art

**Flotation chemistry.** Research focus is on (1) modified/mixed fatty-acid and novel chelating collectors (e.g., cupferron's Ca–O five-membered chelate for fluorite vs. dolomite; bio-derived collectors from acidic olive oil, [US11638923](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/11638923)) and (2) selective inorganic depressants replacing quebracho/lignosulfonate. Acidized water glass (AWG) nearly fully depresses calcite at pH ~8 with sodium oleate while leaving fluorite/barite floatable, via H2SiO4²⁻/H3SiO4⁻ chemisorption on calcite ([Minerals Engineering 2013](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0892687513000745); [MDPI Minerals 2025](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/15/10/1020)); Al2(SO4)3 + AWG mixtures extend this to fluorite–calcite–celestite systems ([Minerals Engineering 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0892687519301700)). Substituting sodium naphthenate for fatty acids lowers required slurry temperature — heated-pulp flotation is a real cost in cold climates ([ResearchGate AWG paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271560207_Flotation_of_fluorite_from_ores_by_using_acidized_water_glass_as_depressant)). "Environmentally friendly" acidspar flowsheets are an explicit research goal ([J. Cleaner Production 2018](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959652618307790)).

**Pre-concentration.** XRT sensor-based sorting is now commercial for fluorspar: TOMRA XRT sorters were installed late 2024 at Koura's Las Cuevas (world's largest fluorspar mine) to strip silica/carbonate ahead of milling ([TOMRA](https://www.tomra.com/mining/media-center/news/2024/koura-flourspar-mine)); sorting-plus-flotation is described as near-mandatory for >95% grades ([MDPI Minerals sorting review](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/9/9/523)). DMS pre-concentration is proven at Canada Fluorspar's St. Lawrence (crush → DMS → grind → sulphide float → fluorite float; 200,000 tpa acidspar design) ([OneMine project description](https://onemine.org/documents/canada-fluorspar-nl-inc-st-lawrence-fluorspar-project-project-description); [IMFORMED](https://imformed.com/fluorspar-revival-in-newfoundland/)), while SepFluor's Nokeng uses HPGR to −6 mm feeding mill/float for 180,000 tpa acidspar + 30,000 tpa metspar from 630,000 tpa ROM ([African Mining](https://www.africanmining.co.za/2019/09/30/nokeng-mining-fluorspar-hills/); [Nokeng fact sheet](https://www.sepfluor.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/nokeng-august-fact-sheet-2018.pdf)).

**Column flotation and fines.** Column flotation is used to upgrade fluorspar byproduct streams from REE carbonatites (Ashram, Quebec), with reported optimization lifting recovery to ~86.8% and grade from 76.5% to 88.6% in test work ([Mining, Metallurgy & Exploration 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42461-022-00666-1); [Minerals Engineering mass-balance study 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0892687521003514); [Auralia/Ashram test program](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/276416/Auralia-Metallurgy-Commences-Flotation-Test-Work-Optimisation-Program-for-the-Ashram-REE-and-Fluorspar-Project-Quebec)). For −20 µm fluorite, liquid–gas microdispersion membrane devices achieved flotation with little/no depressant ([Sep. Purif. Technol. 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1383586620324552)), and nanobubble conditioning boosts fine-particle recovery up to ~25% while cutting collector/frother 50–60% ([ScienceDirect 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2950555025000084)).

**Tailings retreat.** Fluorite flotation tailings retreat (re-flotation, gravity, plus ceramics/building-material uses) is an active review topic ([Int. J. Miner. Metall. Mater. 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12613-025-3208-5); [Xinhai overview](https://www.xinhaimining.com/newo/fluorite-tailings-treatment-efficiency-and-sustainability.html)); barite recovery from Pb-Zn-type tailings uses combined gravity + flotation ([JXSC/industry practice](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-realize-lead-zinc-tailings-reprocessing-dry-stacking-)).

### Named patents/players

- [US11478801B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11478801B2/en) (Institute of Multipurpose Utilization of Mineral Resources, CAGS; filed 2021-01-26, granted 2022-10-25): jig gravity pre-concentration of barite + color sorting of calcite + flotation using acidized water glass / fulvic acid / sodium naphthalene sulfonate depressant blend → ≥98% CaF2 from calcite-rich low-grade fluorite-barite ore. Closest modern art to an integrated tailings-style flowsheet.
- Legacy US art (mostly expired — freedom to operate, but also prior art): [US4043902](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/4043902) (carboxylated fatty-acid aspartate collectors), [US4288315](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/4288315) (fluorspar beneficiation), [US4136019](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/4136019) (co-production of high-purity fluorspar + barite concentrates from complex ore), [US5130037](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/5130037) (salt-type mineral flotation compositions).
- Recent: [US11638923](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/11638923) (olive-oil-derived flotation reagents); large CN family on fluorite depressants/collectors, e.g. [CN102921551B](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN102921551B/en). Downstream: [US5047226](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5047226A/en) / [EP0280439A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP0280439A1/en) (low-As HF manufacture).
- Players: TOMRA (XRT sorting), Koura/Orbia (Las Cuevas), Canada Fluorspar, SepFluor, Ares Strategic Mining, Hastie Mining (Rosiclare), Tertiary Minerals ([Storuman](https://www.tertiaryminerals.com/storuman-fluorspar-project-sweden)), Commerce Resources (Ashram).

### Gaps & pain points (candidate white space)

1. **Calcite selectivity without quebracho/heated pulp**: no US patent family covers AWG-type inorganic depressant blends applied at ambient temperature to *carbonate-hosted* (Mississippi Valley-type) fluorite — IKF ore is limestone-hosted, the hardest selectivity case ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11771-026-6220-7)). Coupling flotation-pulp heating to on-site CHP/waste heat is unpatented integration space.
2. **Legacy tailings retreat as a multi-commodity flowsheet**: IKF tailings carry fluorite + sphalerite (Ge-bearing) + galena + barite + Cd/Pb liabilities ([ATSDR](https://www.atsdr.cdc.gov/HAC/pha/MinervaMine1/MinervaMineHC050407.pdf); [KGS](https://www.uky.edu/KGS/news/2022_critical_mineral.php)); an integrated gravity/sorter/column-flotation retreat that simultaneously produces acidspar, Zn-Ge concentrate, barite, and a stabilized (heavy-metal-immobilized) residue has no identified patent equivalent — US11478801B2 is the nearest art and targets ROM ore, not weathered tailings.
3. **Fine/slimed fluorite recovery**: nanobubble + microdispersion flotation of legacy slimes (<20 µm, where historic gravity mills lost most fluorite) is demonstrated academically but not commercialized or patented for tailings feeds ([S1383586620324552](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1383586620324552)).
4. **Water circuit**: closed-loop process water with residual fatty-acid/depressant accumulation degrades fluorite flotation ([water re-use review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0892687589900666)); biochar/activated-carbon polishing of flotation recycle water (direct synergy with the pyrolysis stream) is an obvious, apparently unclaimed coupling.
5. **Arsenic/heavy-metal management upstream of HF**: spec-driven (10–12 ppm As) removal at the concentrate stage, rather than in HF distillation ([US5047226](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5047226A/en)), e.g., sorbent or sorting-based As rejection, is thin in the art.
6. **Sensor sorting of tailings/coarse dumps**: XRT is proven on ROM fluorspar ([TOMRA/Koura](https://im-mining.com/2024/11/19/tomra-mining-to-provide-xrt-sorting-tech-for-koura-las-cuevas-fluorspar-mine/)) but not claimed for re-mining IKF-type gravel/jig dumps, nor combined with laser-induced fluorescence (fluorite's namesake property) — a distinctive, defensible sorting modality for this district.
